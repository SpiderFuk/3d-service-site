## Problem Statement

3D printing services lack an interactive, modern web presence. Customers can't easily visualize what they'll receive or quickly get quotes for their 3D printing needs.

## Target Users

### Primary: End Consumers
People looking for 3D printing services in Uruguay. They want custom gifts like portraits or need STL/3MF files printed. They may not be tech-savvy and need a visual, intuitive way to understand the service.

### Secondary: Makers with 3D Files
Users who already have STL/3MF files and want them printed. They need a straightforward way to upload their models, preview them, and get a quote.

### Future: B2B Customers
Small businesses needing prototypes or custom parts. They already have their own 3D files and need reliable printing services.

## Key Goals

1. **Showcase services and enable easy quotation** - Primary conversion funnel: browse services and models, then get a quote via WhatsApp
