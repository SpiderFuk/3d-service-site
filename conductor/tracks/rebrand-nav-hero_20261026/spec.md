## Summary

Build sections 01 Navegación (sticky bar, current-section marking, phone menu panel) and 02 Hero (`#inicio`, hours-driven headline, spool art with extrusion, claim tiles) from `design/landing-v1/BUILD.md`.

## Context

The hero quote button and the bar quote button share one rule: only one is visible at a time. The bar button appears after the hero button scrolls out of view. The hero headline swaps between an open and a closed version from the hours setting.

## User Story

As a visitor, I want a clear bar that shows where I am and a hero that tells me what printo does and how to ask for a price, so that I can get a quote in one tap from anywhere on the page.
